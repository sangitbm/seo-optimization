# How to Use the QR Code Generator — Complete Guide

QR codes have experienced a massive renaissance since 2020. They've become a standard tool for bridging offline experiences with online content — and when used strategically, they can be a powerful SEO and marketing asset.

## What Is a QR Code?

A QR (Quick Response) code is a two-dimensional barcode that stores information in a matrix of black and white squares. When scanned with a smartphone camera, it instantly directs users to a URL, displays text, connects to WiFi, initiates a phone call, or performs dozens of other actions.

Unlike traditional barcodes, QR codes:
- Store up to 4,296 characters of data
- Can be scanned from any orientation
- Have built-in error correction (up to 30% of the code can be damaged and still work)
- Work with every modern smartphone without a dedicated app

## Why QR Codes Matter for SEO and Marketing

### Offline-to-Online Traffic
QR codes convert physical touchpoints — print ads, product packaging, business cards, event signage, restaurant menus — into web traffic. This traffic often arrives with high intent since the user made a deliberate choice to scan.

### Trackable with UTM Parameters
By using a URL with UTM parameters in your QR code, you can track exactly how much traffic comes from each physical placement:

`https://example.com/landing-page?utm_source=flyer&utm_medium=print&utm_campaign=summer2026`

This attribution data helps you measure offline marketing ROI and optimize your campaigns.

### Local SEO Impact
For local businesses, QR codes on physical materials (menus, receipts, signage) that link to your Google Business Profile review page can drive review acquisition — one of the strongest local ranking signals.

## How to Use the QR Code Generator

### Step 1: Enter Your URL or Text
Paste your target URL or enter the text you want the QR code to encode. For marketing use, always use a full URL with UTM parameters.

### Step 2: Customize Colors
Choose a foreground (dark) color and background (light) color. Maintain sufficient contrast for reliable scanning — QR code scanners need clear contrast between modules and background.

**Important**: Never invert a QR code (light on dark) without testing it thoroughly. Many scanners struggle with inverted QR codes.

### Step 3: Generate and Preview
Click "Generate QR Code" and preview the result. Scan it with your phone to verify it works correctly before printing or publishing.

### Step 4: Download
Download as:
- **PNG**: For digital use, email, social media, websites
- **SVG**: For print use (infinitely scalable without quality loss)

**Always use SVG for print materials** to ensure crisp, clear QR codes at any size.

## QR Code Size and Placement Guidelines

For reliable scanning, QR codes must be a minimum size. A general rule is the **10:1 ratio** — the scan distance should be no more than 10x the QR code size:

| QR Code Size | Max Scan Distance |
|-------------|-------------------|
| 2.5 cm (1") | 25 cm (10") |
| 5 cm (2") | 50 cm (20") |
| 10 cm (4") | 100 cm (39") |

For most printed materials, a minimum size of **2.5 × 2.5 cm** is recommended.

## QR Code Use Cases for Businesses

### Physical Marketing
- **Business cards**: Link to your portfolio or LinkedIn profile
- **Flyers and brochures**: Drive traffic to a specific landing page
- **Product packaging**: Link to product instructions, videos, or registration pages
- **Event banners**: Link to event schedules or registration forms

### In-Store and Hospitality
- **Restaurant menus**: Replace physical menus with digital ones
- **Product displays**: Link to more information or online purchase options
- **Receipts**: Link to review platforms or loyalty programs

### Real Estate and Property
- **For-sale signs**: Link to virtual tours
- **Open house materials**: Link to floor plans and listing details

## Static vs. Dynamic QR Codes

Our generator creates **static QR codes** — the URL is encoded directly into the QR code pattern. Once generated, the destination cannot be changed without creating a new QR code.

**Dynamic QR codes** (from paid services) use a redirect service, allowing you to change the destination URL after printing. Use these for campaigns where you might need to update the link later.

## QR Code Best Practices

1. **Always test before printing**: Scan with multiple devices and QR code apps
2. **Add a call to action**: "Scan to learn more" or "Scan to get 20% off"
3. **Include a URL fallback**: Print the URL below the QR code for non-smartphone users
4. **Ensure landing page is mobile-optimized**: 100% of QR code traffic is mobile
5. **Use UTM parameters**: Track every QR code placement separately
